# EA Nerd

### Software engineers with executive assistant skills.

EA Nerd trains, certifies, and deploys software engineers who combine strong technical execution with operational excellence.

We believe modern businesses don't just need more developers—they need engineers who can own systems, automate workflows, communicate effectively, and execute with the reliability of a trusted executive assistant.

---

## What We Do

We help businesses extend their engineering capacity with professionals who can:

- Build and maintain modern software
- Develop AI-powered solutions and automations
- Improve internal tools and business workflows
- Integrate APIs and third-party platforms
- Support technical operations with ownership and accountability

Every engineer goes through practical technical evaluation and operational training before joining client engagements.

---

## Engineering Principles

- Build practical solutions
- Communicate clearly
- Automate repetitive work
- Think like owners
- Ship reliable software
- Security by default
